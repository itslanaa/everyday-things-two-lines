Entry for the Make.fun bounty "Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run".

`main.ts` writes a two-line explanation to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A toaster is a tiny sun trapped in a box: it browns your bread the way the sun browns your skin.
>
> The analogy works because both use heat to change colour, but it leaves out that the sun never pops up when it is done.

Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old in a single vivid sentence built on an analogy. Line 2 says why that analogy works: what it maps (heat changing colour) and what it leaves out (the sun never pops up when it is done).
